Heaven Furniture Mart is one of Chattogram's leading bespoke furniture brands. We design and craft custom furniture — sofas, beds, dining sets, office pieces — built around what a customer actually wants, not pulled off a shelf.

Alongside the furniture we handle interior styling, so a room arrives finished rather than assembled. Everything runs out of our own workshop and showroom on Agrabad Access Road.
